伝統と真心 • TRADITION & PASSION

Rooted in Victoria's Craft Since 2007

For nearly two decades, Chiba Sushi has been a cherished culinary staple in Victoria, British Columbia. In June 2021, we welcomed our loyal guests to our spacious, warm modern dining room in the heart of Quadra Village at 2630 Quadra Street.

Our sushi chefs combine time-honored Edo Japanese techniques with British Columbia's richest coastal bounty—featuring pristine wild Pacific sockeye salmon, tender albacore tuna, and sweet botan ebi. Every piece of fish is sliced with reverence, every roll flame-seared with artisanal glazes, and every broth slow-simmered in house.

Wild Coastal Catch

Prioritizing sustainable B.C. wild salmon, albacore tuna, and real Dungeness crab.

Aburi Flame Craft

Flame-torched pressed box sushi (Oshi) with secret reductions and serrano accents.

Warm Izakaya Comforts

Sizzling teriyaki platters, crisp tempura, handmade gyoza, and comforting udon.

Licensed Sake & Drinks

Chilled Junmai Ginjo sake, Asahi draft beer, local wines, and roasted genmaicha.

Selected "The Best Sushi Restaurant in Victoria, BC"

Honored by Quality Business Awards 2023 for excellence in fish freshness, hospitality, and outstanding customer feedback.
